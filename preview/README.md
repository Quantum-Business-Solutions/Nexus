# Nexus Network Technologies: website preview

Three complete, clickable website directions for Nexus Network Technologies, prepared by Quantum Business Solutions,
served under `/preview/` of the Nexus repo at https://nexus-gold-beta.vercel.app/preview/ (noindex header set in the repo's `vercel.json`).

Generated by `scripts/preview.py` in `Quantum-Business-Solutions/websitedesign` from `brands/nexus-network-technologies.content.json`,
with `brands/nexus-network-technologies.seo.json` and `.audit.json` for the search package. Regenerate with:

```bash
cd websitedesign && python3 scripts/preview.py --content brands/nexus-network-technologies.content.json \
  --themes "Quantum Showcase,Quantum Clean,Quantum Press" --recommend "Quantum Showcase" \
  --roles "Showcase: the network made visible, dark layered hero, 3D closet, the most sales-forward|Clean: If enterprise means restraint: white ground, humanist sans, hairline cards, the proof doing all the talking. The fastest and quietest of the three.|Press: The Connective as an editorial site: warm paper, a serif, founder story high on the page. The most human of the three and the least like any MSP in the Carolinas." \
  --base-url https://nexus-gold-beta.vercel.app/preview --out ../nexus/preview
```

- `index.html`: the hub. The three directions, our pick, side by side, search and AI answers with the ranked findings, every page of nexusnt.com scored against its rebuilt page, what we heard, to confirm, every page, the plan.
- `showcase/`, `clean/`, `press/`: one folder per direction, 21 pages each, with a direction switcher on every page.
- `seo-report.html`: the full search and AI-answer analysis of nexusnt.com. `seo-audit-pages.csv`: every page and every check. `redirects.csv`: the 301 map. `seo-build-scores.json`: the build scored by the same script.
- `design-system.html`: tokens, type, modules and voice, generated from the same source as the pages.
- `standard.html`: the Quantum Website Standard, the client-facing guarantee.
- `assets/`: logo, generated renders and the 3D model, labelled as generated on the pages.

Nothing here is live or indexed. No form submits anywhere. No analytics. The site itself is built in HubSpot after a direction is chosen.
