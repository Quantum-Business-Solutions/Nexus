Social and the entity

What connects the company across the web

Answer engines corroborate a company across the web before they name it. For Nexus the corroboration points the wrong way: the name is shared with dozens of unrelated companies, and the site does nothing to disambiguate.

6 of 10brand review results that belong to other companies named Nexus
1social profile linked from the site (LinkedIn), under a handle that differs from the name
0sameAs, address or phone in the Organization schema; the WebSite schema reads Just another WordPress site
3chambers linking to the site (Triangle East, Charlotte Area, Greensboro), the only real referring domains

What we found

  • The Organization schema is Yoast's default: name, URL and logo. Without sameAs, address or phone, an engine cannot tell this Nexus from Nexus Technologies in Glassdoor's index or Nexus Networks LTD in Trustpilot's.
  • The LinkedIn page exists and is active but its handle is nexus-networking-technologies. The site should link it and the schema should name it, so the two are tied together.
  • No Google Business Profile appeared in any query. For a company with demonstration rooms in Morrisville, the profile, its photographs, Q&A and reviews are the largest local answer-engine input, and they are not being fed.
  • The client comments page is real proof from post-ticket surveys and ranks first for the brand reviews query. It has no Review schema and cannot have any until the reviews live on a third-party platform.
  • The chambers are the only links a human placed. They carry the right name, address and phone and are the model for every listing that follows.

What we would do

Build Organization schema with legalName, the Morrisville address, telephone, sameAs to LinkedIn and Glassdoor, areaServed for Raleigh, Durham, Cary and Morrisville, and the office as a LocalBusiness with opening hours and coordinates.

Build Nexus Network Technologies or Raleigh-Durham in every title, and a facts block on the about page: founder, founded, founder-owned, Morrisville, the eight service lines.

30 days Claim and complete the Google Business Profile: hours, services, photographs of the demonstration rooms, Q&A seeded from the site FAQ, and the review request added to the post-ticket survey.

30 days Cloudtango, Clutch, CloudSecureTech and The Manifest profiles with the same name, address and phone; the LinkedIn page linked from the footer.

90 days A YouTube channel with the demonstration rooms and the founder story, linked from the site with VideoObject schema; vendor partner locators for the brands Nexus resells.
