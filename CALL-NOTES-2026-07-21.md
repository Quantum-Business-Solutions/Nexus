## What Nexus actually told us
- **Strategy over visuals.** Shane: "I'm really more focused on the strategy portion… a roadmap of items. Less about the final product at this point." Lead with the plan; the five themes are a menu, not the pitch.
- **The one idea: Nexus = the connection.** Lin: "Our website needs to convey the guiding principle… Nexus. It's the connection point for technologies… the connection between us and the client. It's a bond. A relationship. A win-win, not win-lose."
- **Not PE-backed.** Shane: "It's gotta come through that we're not PE-backed." Founder-owned, ~30 years, protective/partnership-first.
- **Selective, less-is-more.** Would rather have 60–80 great-fit clients than 200 and be sloppy.
- **Owner (Mike) is the decision.** He's the gatekeeper and wasn't on the call. Lin: he has "angst" over past business-development spend with no ROI; he needs "a very strong plan that guarantees success and a true return on investment in a reasonable amount of time."
- **Systemize the message everywhere** — brochures, business cards, email signatures, hold music, invoices, paid ads, proposals.

- Ground everything in **their market** (Triangle / Carolinas), not stock personas.
- For the real build: real photography of their team/region, real client quotes, real numbers. Avoid the "five identical themes" feel — present the recommended direction first, others as options.
